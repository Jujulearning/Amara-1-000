Our approach

A new approach to maternal nutrition.

Our mission To demystify nutrition for every mother, in every kitchen, in every culture.

Amara is in the concept and early prototype stage.

Four connected parts, designed together: nutrition guidance that starts from familiar foods, supported by technology and grounded in evidence.

01

Culturally Inclusive Nutrition

Nutrition guidance that recognizes familiar foods, dietary traditions, and the diversity of maternal experiences.

02

Personalized Digital Support

Our envisioned platform will help mothers understand nutritional needs while accounting for their food preferences and circumstances.

03

Evidence-Informed Guidance

Nutrition information designed to complement professional care and support informed decisions.

04

The First 1,000 Days

Support across pregnancy, infancy, and early childhood, with attention to the changing nutritional needs of mothers and children.
