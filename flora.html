Flora · AI nutrition advisor

Meet Flora.

Nutrition guidance that understands your food.

Flora is Amara’s envisioned AI-powered nutrition advisor, designed to help mothers explore nutrition information through the foods, cultures, and traditions they know.

How Flora could help

  1. 01Understanding the nutritional composition of familiar meals.
  2. 02Exploring food choices during pregnancy and postpartum.
  3. 03Accessing culturally relevant nutrition information.
  4. 04Supporting informed conversations with healthcare professionals.

A note on Flora. Flora shares general nutrition information. It does not diagnose or monitor medical conditions, and it does not replace your doctor, midwife, or dietitian.
