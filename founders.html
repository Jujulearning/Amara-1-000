Black American food traditions

Erica Stephens, MPH

Co-founder · MPH, Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health

Erica grew up on a tobacco farm in rural Virginia, eating the foods of the South. Collard greens slow-cooked with smoked turkey. Black-eyed peas, sweet potatoes, butter beans. Food grown, shared, and passed down through generations.

Her love of health, nutrition, fitness, and motherhood eventually became part of her work in public health. At Hopkins, she focused on health systems, policy, data, and digital health. She co-founded Amara Health with one conviction: nutrition tools should reflect the foods, cultures, and knowledge women already bring to the table.
