Privacy Notice

Your information, handled with care.

Amara Health is an early-stage company. This notice explains what information this website collects, why, and what we do with it. Last updated: September 2026.

What we collect

  • Waitlist: your email address and your consent to hear from us.
  • Partnership inquiries: your name, work email, organization, organization type, any message you choose to send, and your consent for us to respond.

We do not ask for health information through this website. Please do not include personal medical details in your message.

How we use it

  • To let you know when early access to Amara opens, and to share occasional product updates.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers who help us operate this website and receive form submissions, and only for those purposes.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any message we send, or ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you have shared by replying to any email from us.

This website does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering them.

Always seek the guidance of your doctor, midwife, or other qualified health provider with questions about your health or your child's health. If you think you may have a medical emergency, call 911.
